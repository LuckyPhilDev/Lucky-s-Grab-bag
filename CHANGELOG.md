## [1.13.0] - 2026-06-11

### Added
- **Power Infusion Picker**: priests now get a small window in parties and raids to choose who receives their Power Infusion. Click a name and the 'PI' macro updates to cast on that player, no macro editing needed. Drop the macro on your action bar once and pick new targets any time.
- **Auto Combat Logging**: optionally starts combat logging when you enter selected raid difficulties or a Mythic+ key, and stops it when you leave. Current season only by default.
