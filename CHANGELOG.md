## [Unreleased]

### Added

- **Nemesis Flute Reminder** Reach a delve's respawn point without this week's Trovehunter's Bounty and your Scalebound Herald's Flute pops up, ready to summon the Nemesis. Uses the same minimum delve level as the map.

### Improved

- **Delve Buttons** The Trovehunter's Bounty, flute and Dundun buttons now sit together in one row and move as one.

- **Combat Prep Window** Hovering the bar shows a lock tab above its corner. Click it to lock the bar in place so a stray right-drag can't move it.
- **Use Items Popup** Chest of Gold and Rattling Bag of Gold now get a button.

## [1.32.0] - 2026-09-28

### Added

- **Assign Tanks** A button on the Combat Prep window in raids sets everyone in a tank role as a main tank in one click.

### Improved

- **Combat Prep Window** Redesigned as a compact bar of icons. While a pull or break is counting down, its button turns into a red cross that cancels it, whoever started it.
