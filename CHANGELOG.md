## [1.12.0] - 2026-06-05

### Added
- **Concentration on Profession Book** — see your Concentration for both professions at a glance in the Profession Book, with a bar above each skill bar. No need to open each profession. Hover to see how long until it refills.

### Fixed
- Minimap button now appears correctly on the minimap instead of sometimes hiding behind it.
- Removed a black square that could appear when hovering the minimap button.

## [1.10.0] - 2026-05-24

### Added
- **Spend to Next Perk** — clicking a profession specialisation node now keeps spending knowledge points until the next perk threshold, so you don't have to click five times for each tier.
- **1s Tip on Alt Work Orders** — when you submit a personal work order to one of your own characters, the tip is set to 1 silver automatically so you don't have to clear it each time.
