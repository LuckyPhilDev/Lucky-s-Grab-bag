## [Unreleased]

### Added
- **Use Items Popup** Add any item you like from **Your items…** in settings, by typing its ID or dragging it in from your bags. Give it a count for items used in batches, and the button waits until you hold that many. `/grabbag items add`, `remove` and `list` do the same from chat.

### Improved
- **Use Items Popup** Garrison Salvage Yard bags, the Sack of Salvaged Goods and Large Crate of Salvage, now get a button while you are in your garrison.

## [1.37.1] - 2026-10-16

### Improved
- **Auto-enter Labyrinth Chambers** The door to the Speaker's Chamber, the final challenge, now takes you straight through as well.

## [1.37.0] - 2026-10-16

### Added
- **Corrosive Codex Loadouts** A panel docked to the Corrosive Codex saves the powers you have picked as named loadouts, shared across your account. Click a loadout to swap your powers over to it.
- **Auto-enter Labyrinth Chambers** Opening a door to the next chamber of a Labyrinth takes you straight through, without the confirmation.
