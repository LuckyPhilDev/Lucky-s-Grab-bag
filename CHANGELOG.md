## [1.5.0] - 2026-05-01

### Added
- **Reagent Mains** — assign each reagent category (Herbs, Cloth, Gems, etc.) to a designated main. When you open the warband bank on a different character, reagents in those categories are deposited automatically. Existing assignments from Warband Stockist are imported on first run.

### Improved
- Redesigned settings panel — features are grouped into sections in the left sidebar, with hover descriptions, status, and screenshots in a new About panel on the right.
