## [Unreleased]

### Added
- **Corrosive Codex Loadouts** A panel docked to the Corrosive Codex, in its own style, saves the powers you have picked as a named loadout. Click a saved loadout to swap your powers over to it, or right-click to delete it. Loadouts are shared across your account.

## [1.36.0] - 2026-10-15

### Added
- **Bulk Buy** BuyEmAll's shift-click window, built in and updated for Midnight. Shift-click an item at a vendor to buy any amount, with Stack and Max buttons and a check before anything over one stack.
- **Legendary Rank 5 Curios** Rank 5 curios now show orange, like legendary items, in your delve companion's curio list, your bags (Baganator included) and their tooltips, instead of the same purple as rank 4.

### Fixed
- The Use Items Popup now shows a button for the Satchel of Corrosive Coins.
