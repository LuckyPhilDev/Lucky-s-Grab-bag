## [1.18.0] - 2026-07-04

### Added
- **Housing rotate buttons**: while editing a house in Expert Mode with a piece of decor selected, a floating panel offers buttons to rotate it by an exact number of degrees, plus a reset button. A running total shows how far it's turned since the last reset, handy for lining a rug up square with a wall. Right-click drag to move the panel. Off by default, enable it in Settings under Housing.
