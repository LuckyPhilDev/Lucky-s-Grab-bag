## [Unreleased]

### Added
- **Boons Panel** Shows the stats your delve Boons buff grants in a small panel, so you no longer have to find the buff and hover it. Only stats you have collected are listed, and a stat shimmers when it goes up. Loa-Spirit conduit buffs in the Labyrinth of Kindo'jan are listed too, with a countdown of their two minutes. The game hides aura details in combat, so the panel dims until combat ends. Boons that raise health, movement speed, your primary stat or damage reduction still count up as you pick them up; the others show once combat ends. Click the X to turn it off; turn it back on in settings under Delves.

## [1.38.0] - 2026-10-26

### Added
- **Use Items Popup** Add any item you like from **Your items…** in settings, by typing its ID or dragging it in from your bags. Give it a count for items used in batches, and the button waits until you hold that many. `/grabbag items add`, `remove` and `list` do the same from chat.
- **Wago Addons** Lucky's Grab-bag is now published on Wago Addons too, so you can install and update it with the Wago app.

### Improved
- **Use Items Popup** Garrison Salvage Yard bags, the Sack of Salvaged Goods and Large Crate of Salvage, now get a button while you are in your garrison.
- **Use Items Popup** Mistcrest bags, from the Bundle of Adventurer Mistcrests up to the Glorious Cluster of Myth Mistcrests, now get a button.
